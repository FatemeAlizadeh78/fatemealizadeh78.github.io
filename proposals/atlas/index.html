01 / Scientific information extraction

Teaching language models to return structure.

Can task decomposition make scientific entity and relation extraction more reliable?

Compared prompting and LoRA adaptation of Qwen, Llama, and Gemma on SciER, then paired Gemma entity generation with a typed-pair SciBERT relation classifier. The work investigates exact spans, directed relations, JSON reliability, and out-of-domain behavior.

LoRA fine-tuningGemma + SciBERT854 test sentences

Saved results and the test-selected relation threshold are explained in the case study.

02 / Time-series forecasting

Looking 1,439 hours ahead.

How do you preserve daily, weekly, and annual patterns across a long forecast horizon?

Studied hourly pedestrian counts using dynamic harmonic regression with ARIMA errors, structural unobserved-components models, and direct machine-learning estimators. Annual-lag features and season-matched backtests connect the models to the data’s temporal structure.

ARIMA + UCMGradient boostingSeasonal backtesting

Validation scores and pooled-calibration limits are documented alongside the stricter audit.

03 / Financial Markets Analytics

Allocating under uncertainty.

What changes when portfolio estimates and investor views both carry uncertainty?

Built a rolling 20-ETF experiment with 60-month estimation windows, constrained Markowitz optimization, Michaud resampling, and Black–Litterman momentum views. Compared allocation stability and historical risk–return behavior over 160 out-of-sample months.

Constrained optimizationBayesian views20 multi-asset ETFs

Arithmetic annualization and a zero risk-free rate; costs are excluded. This is separate from the 2025 S&P 500 portfolio study.
